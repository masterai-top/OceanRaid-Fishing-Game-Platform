Responsible use

Fairness, security and compliance

Fair play

Do not manipulate individual player outcomes or misrepresent probabilities and rewards. Keep hits, balances and settlement authoritative on reviewed servers and maintain tamper-evident audit logs.

Security and privacy

Use least privilege, strong authentication, encryption, rate limiting, secret rotation, data minimization, retention controls, monitoring and tested incident response.

Markets and platforms

Obtain target-market review for game classification, competitions, prizes, payments, virtual items, probability disclosure, age limits, privacy, taxation and consumer protection. Review Apple, Google, payment and advertising policies separately.

Public source access and product screenshots do not constitute legal approval, production certification or a revenue guarantee.
